Terms of Service

What the app is

Clips elgab3n is a personal desktop tool that runs on a single Windows computer owned by the streamer elgab3n. It turns recordings of elgab3n's own Twitch streams into short vertical clips, lets elgab3n review each clip, and publishes only the clips he approves to his own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. The only user is elgab3n, who signs in to his own accounts through the official Instagram and TikTok login screens. Nobody else can create an account in it.

Content

  • The app only processes recordings of elgab3n's own streams. It does not download or reuse content from other creators.
  • Nothing is posted without elgab3n approving the clip first. Before posting to TikTok he chooses the privacy level, interaction settings and commercial content disclosure, and accepts TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
  • elgab3n is responsible for the clips he publishes and for following the Instagram and TikTok community guidelines.

Third-party platforms

Publishing uses the official Instagram API and the TikTok Content Posting API. Use of those platforms is governed by their own terms. Access can be revoked at any time from the Instagram or TikTok app settings.

Privacy Policy

Data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and creator posting settings, used to show which account will receive the post and to publish the approved video.
  • Instagram: the account ID and username of @elgab3n, used to publish approved Reels.
  • Stream recordings: elgab3n's own videos, processed on his computer.

How it is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored only on elgab3n's computer. There is no server, no database in the cloud and no analytics. The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other person.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared for advertising. The only outside services involved are Instagram and TikTok (to publish) and the Anthropic API, which receives short text transcripts of elgab3n's own stream to suggest titles, descriptions and hashtags.

Deleting data

Disconnecting the app in Instagram (Settings › Apps and websites) or TikTok (Settings › Security › Apps) revokes its access immediately. Deleting the app's folder on the computer removes all stored tokens and clips.

Contact

Questions about this app: send a direct message to @elgab3n on Instagram or TikTok.
